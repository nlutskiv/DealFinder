Privacy Policy

DealFinder currently does not require users to create an account. Basic technical logs may be collected to keep the service running, prevent abuse and improve product ranking quality.

Search and listing interactions may be processed by the backend to return relevant results and to avoid repeated AI analysis of the same listing.

If affiliate links are used, the retailer or affiliate network may process click and purchase data according to their own privacy policies.

Affiliate disclosure

DealFinder may earn a commission when users click product links and make a purchase. This does not directly determine the ranking score. Rankings are intended to reflect value, risk and listing quality.
