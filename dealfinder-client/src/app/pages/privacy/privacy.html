Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DealFinder ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use this website. We are committed to handling your data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Data controller

DealFinder is operated as an independent web service. For data-related enquiries, contact us at lutskiv.nazar1@gmail.com.

What data we collect

DealFinder does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses or payment information directly. The following technical data may be collected automatically:

  • Server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamp)
  • Search queries submitted within the site
  • Error and performance telemetry used to maintain service quality

This data is used solely for operating, securing and improving the service. It is not sold to third parties.

Cookies

DealFinder uses only essential session cookies required for the website to function. We do not currently use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated and a cookie consent notice will be displayed.

Affiliate links and third-party data

DealFinder participates in affiliate programmes, including the Awin affiliate network. When you click a product link, you will be redirected to a retailer's website via an affiliate tracking link. At that point, the retailer and/or affiliate network (e.g. Awin) may set their own cookies and collect data according to their own privacy policies. DealFinder is not responsible for third-party data practices.

You can review Awin's privacy policy at awin.com/gb/legal/privacy-policy.

AI processing

Product listing text (titles, descriptions, prices) is processed by an AI model to generate quality and risk scores. This processing does not involve personal user data. Listing analysis results are cached to reduce repeated processing.

Data retention

Server access logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes. Cached AI analysis results are retained until the associated listing is removed from the database.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify or erase personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. Since DealFinder does not collect personal accounts, the only personal data we may hold is within server access logs. To exercise your rights, contact us at lutskiv.nazar1@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Affiliate disclosure

DealFinder may earn a commission when users click product links and make a purchase. This commission is paid by the retailer and does not affect the price you pay. It does not influence product rankings.
