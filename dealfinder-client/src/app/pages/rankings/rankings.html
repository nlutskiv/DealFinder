How rankings work

DealFinder scores listings using several independent signals. The score is designed to help users compare value and risk, not to guarantee product quality.

Product match

Checks whether the listing matches the selected component model.

Value

Compares the listing price against similar listings and market context.

Condition

Rewards clear testing evidence and penalizes vague or faulty listings.

Seller trust

Considers seller feedback, return policy, receipts and buyer protection.

Risk

Flags warning signs such as mining use, no display, bent pins, no returns, untested parts or repair-only listings.

AI analysis is used as guidance only. Always check the seller, listing details and return policy before buying.
