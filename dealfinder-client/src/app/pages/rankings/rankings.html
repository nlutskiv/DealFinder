How rankings work

Performance per pound

GPUs and CPUs are ranked by a relative performance index divided by the live lowest price. Best value wins, not just the cheapest old part.

Total price (lowest first)

Other components are sorted by item price plus shipping cost, so the true cheapest option always appears first.

Retailer feed quality

Only listings from active retailer feeds are shown. Stale or unavailable listings are excluded when feeds refresh.

No paid placement

Retailers can't pay for a higher position. Rankings go by price and performance only.

Prices come from retailer feeds and may not always match live stock. Check the retailer's site before buying.
