How rankings work

Performance per pound

GPUs and CPUs are ranked by a relative performance index divided by the live lowest price — so the best value part wins, not the cheapest obsolete one.

Total price (lowest first)

Other components are sorted by item price plus shipping cost, so the true cheapest option always appears first.

Retailer feed quality

Only listings from active retailer feeds are shown. Stale or unavailable listings are excluded when feeds refresh.

No paid placement

Retailers cannot pay for a higher position. Rankings are determined entirely by price and performance — nothing else.

Prices and availability are sourced from retailer feeds and may not always reflect real-time stock. Always verify the final price on the retailer's site before purchasing.
