How rankings work

Total price (lowest first)

Listings are sorted by item price plus shipping cost, so the true cheapest option always appears first.

Model match

Results are filtered to the exact component model you selected, so you only see relevant listings.

Retailer feed quality

Only listings from active retailer feeds are shown. Stale or unavailable listings are excluded when feeds refresh.

No paid placement

Retailers cannot pay for a higher position. Rankings are determined entirely by price — nothing else.

Prices and availability are sourced from retailer feeds and may not always reflect real-time stock. Always verify the final price on the retailer's site before purchasing.
