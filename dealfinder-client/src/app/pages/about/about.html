About DealFinder

DealFinder is a UK-based price comparison platform for PC hardware. We aggregate product listings from retailer affiliate feeds and present them sorted by price, so you can instantly see the cheapest option for any component.

Our goal is simple: cut the time it takes to find the best price on a GPU, CPU, or any other PC part. Instead of visiting five retailer sites, you search once and compare in seconds. Every listing links directly to the retailer so you buy with full warranty and buyer protection.

What we cover

Graphics Cards (GPU)

NVIDIA RTX and AMD Radeon listings across all current-generation models.

Processors (CPU)

Intel Core and AMD Ryzen desktop processors.

Memory (RAM)

DDR4 and DDR5 desktop memory kits in all common capacities.

Motherboards

Intel and AMD platform boards across all major chipsets.

Power Supplies (PSU)

ATX and SFX units from 300 W to 1500 W.

Cases & Coolers

Mid-tower, full-tower and ITX cases, plus air and AIO liquid coolers.

How rankings work

Listings for a given model are sorted by total price (item price plus shipping), lowest first. Rankings are independent — merchants cannot purchase higher placement and we have no commercial relationship with any specific retailer that would influence result order.

PC Assembler

Our AI-powered PC Assembler lets you enter a budget and receive a complete, compatible build drawn from live listings. It allocates budget across all eight component categories and verifies socket, memory and power compatibility before recommending a build.

Affiliate disclosure

DealFinder participates in affiliate programmes including the Awin network. When you click a product link and make a purchase, DealFinder may earn a commission from the retailer. This commission does not affect ranking scores or the order in which listings appear.
