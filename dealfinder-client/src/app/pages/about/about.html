About DealFinder

DealFinder helps users compare PC hardware listings and avoid risky used components. It focuses on GPUs, CPUs, RAM, PSUs and motherboards.

The platform ranks listings using price, condition, seller trust and buyer risk. The aim is to make second-hand PC hardware buying easier, safer and more transparent.

DealFinder is currently an early-stage project. Product data, ranking rules and supported categories are actively being improved.
