About DealFinder

DealFinder is a UK price comparison site for PC hardware. We pull listings from retailer feeds and sort them by price, so you can see the cheapest option for any component without visiting five different sites.

The point is to cut the time it takes to find the best price on a GPU, CPU or any other part. You search once and compare in seconds. Every listing links directly to the retailer so you buy with full warranty and buyer protection.

What we cover

Graphics Cards (GPU)

NVIDIA RTX and AMD Radeon listings across all current-generation models.

Processors (CPU)

Intel Core and AMD Ryzen desktop processors.

Memory (RAM)

DDR4 and DDR5 desktop memory kits in all common capacities.

Motherboards

Intel and AMD platform boards across all major chipsets.

Power Supplies (PSU)

ATX and SFX units from 300 W to 1500 W.

Cases & Coolers

Mid-tower, full-tower and ITX cases, plus air and AIO liquid coolers.

How rankings work

Listings for a given model are sorted by total price (item price plus shipping), lowest first. Rankings are independent. Merchants cannot pay for higher placement and we have no commercial relationship with any retailer that would affect result order.

PC Assembler

Enter a budget and get a full parts list from live listings. It splits the budget across eight component categories and checks socket, memory and power compatibility before suggesting anything.

Affiliate disclosure

DealFinder participates in affiliate programmes including the Awin network. When you click a product link and make a purchase, DealFinder may earn a commission from the retailer. This commission does not affect ranking scores or the order in which listings appear.
